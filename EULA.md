## 13. Peers, relays and your data

**Your data stays on your device.** The Client stores your settings, profiles, contacts and keys locally. It sends no telemetry or usage data to us.

**Peers.** You choose whom you pair with and what control you grant them. A Peer's actions are that Peer's responsibility. We do not monitor, moderate or take part in your sessions.

**Relays.** Traffic between you and your Peers passes through a relay chosen by you or your Peer, run by an Operator who may be anyone. That traffic is end-to-end encrypted: no relay can read it. You accept that this information is visible to the Operator of every relay you or your Peers use. We are responsible only for the Official Relay (Part D).
